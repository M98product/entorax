Docs

The short version.

What is Entorax?

An AI co-founder. You describe a project, and four specialised agents help you turn it into a business: research, branding, financial plan, legal setup.

Who are the agents?

Strategist (market and competitors), Designer (name, logo, site), CFO (forecast, break-even, funding) and Legal (company type, steps, documents). The Entorax Team chat coordinates all four.

Does Entorax own my idea?

No. Your project and everything the agents produce for it belong to you.

Why Robinhood Chain?

Each project can get its own wallet to receive payments, pay suppliers and raise from its community, with every movement traceable.

Is this legal or financial advice?

No. The agents give you a solid first draft. Check legal and financial decisions with a qualified professional.
